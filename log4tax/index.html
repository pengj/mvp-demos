Inbox

The review tray — the first of three states: to review → filed → still to come. Documents land here exactly as they arrived. Tap one to check what the app read on this phone and file it. Nothing is filed without you confirming it.

Add a document

Four ways in — each button below drops a fictional sample into the Inbox so you can triage it.

No email inbox on a server. In v1 you forward a document to your own mail app, then share it into Log4Tax — no developer backend ever sees your documents.

Export a pack

The January flow: one ZIP per pack per tax year, for your accountant or your filing tool. In filing season the flow opens on the filing year — the one carrying the 31 January deadline — not the year you are still collecting for.

Find

Collecting is half the product; getting a document back out is the other half. Search everything filed by type, supplier or category — recently filed sits on top, because that is usually what you are after.

Walk the key features

Log4Tax catches tax documents as they arrive through the year, checks them against what should have arrived, and hands over one tidy pack in January. Tap a step to jump to it on the phone.

The boundary, by design: Log4Tax collects documents only — it does not file your return, calculate tax, or give advice. There is no account and no server; documents live on the phone behind Face ID, and the export index is an inventory of what was collected, never a set of tax figures.
